The formal theorem

The independent statement uses finitely generated right modules, represented in Lean as modules over Aᵐᵒᵖ. A complete finite indecomposable family expresses finite representation type. Simple modules are counted directly using Mathlib’s IsSimpleModule.

Special biseriality is expressed by an admissible finite bound-quiver presentation in the Morita class. The two incoming/outgoing arrow bounds and two nonzero-continuation bounds appear explicitly. The presentation uses the endomorphism algebra of the direct sum of representables.

MagnitudeConjecture.Statement.mainClaim proves this statement. Statement.isSpecialBiserial_iff and the numerical comparison lemmas connect it to the library’s definitions.
